About me

I shape how a brand looks, sounds and shows up online.

I’m Zayb, a creative director and social media marketer. For the last 4 years I’ve built brand identities and social feeds for 50+ projects across 10+ industries, from healthcare and real estate to food, education and coffee.

I work in Punjabi, Urdu and English, so your message lands the way your audience actually speaks.

Selected
work

Ten selected brand and social projects across healthcare, real estate, food, education and coffee. Tap any project to see it up close.

  • 01↗
    Social Media · Healthcare

    Quantum Telehealth

    HealthcareSocialContent Calendars

    Quantum needed to feel like a person, not just a paid ad. I built a human-first Instagram system: a brand face, behind-the-scenes posts, interactive stories, content calendars and a brand brief, then the launch identity for a new GP clinic. It beat both April reach targets of 250, reaching 15.6K on Facebook and 961 on Instagram.

  • 02↗
    Social Media · Real Estate

    Meadow City

    SocialUGCEvents

    A housing society needed a social presence people trusted, and a launch for its new block. I led a full social revamp with resident UGC up front and leadership content carrying the founders' vision, then an in-person launch ceremony. It drew 2,000+ attendees, strong bookings and better customer retention.

  • 03↗
    Social Media · Food

    Tawa Chatkhara

    CreatorsVideoSocial

    A well-known fast food chain wanted buzz and orders for a new branch. I built a community of 30+ small creators on barter collaborations, produced stronger videos with better hooks, and branded the juice and Roosters sub-brands. Orders at the branch grew and engagement roughly doubled.

  • 04↗
    Brand Identity · Education

    Education Drive Consultants

    IdentityTemplatesSocial

    A study-abroad consultancy needed to look as premium and trustworthy as the universities it works with. I made a brand guide (logo variations, palette, type, elements, imagery), a landing page and a social template system. The result is one consistent identity across the site and Instagram that the team can reuse on every post.

  • 05↗
    Social Media · Education

    NUML Lahore

    SocialTemplatesRedesign

    A university's feed mixed text-heavy flyers, event photos and announcements in different styles, so nothing stood out. I designed an editorial navy-and-cream grid with one type system, a template for each post type and organised highlights. The result is a calmer, consistent feed built on real students, faculty and campus photography.

  • 06↗
    Social Media · Coffee

    Cafe Noir — Feed

    BrandingSocialPhotography

    A modern coffee brand that speaks in whispers needed a feed as calm as its product. I designed Instagram grid sets with a bold wordmark, flowing black shapes, warm neutral photography and quiet lines like “Sip. Slow. Repeat.” The result is a minimal feed that lets the product breathe.

  • 07↗
    Visual Direction · Guidelines

    Cafe Noir — Visual Direction

    GuidelinesTypographyArt Direction

    The brand needed a rulebook so every post and every cup felt like the same brand. I set the palette, bold and serif type pairing, icons, organic patterns, photography direction and tone, and showed it on signage, cups, packaging and coffee bags. The result is a system the team can follow.

  • 08↗
    Brand Identity · Dental

    Navory

    IdentityHealthcareStationery

    A specialist dental clinic needed an identity that feels gentle and trustworthy, with a feed to match. I designed the logo and a green, cream and blue palette, applied it to uniforms, the clinic interior, a desk calendar and business cards, and laid out a 3x3 Instagram grid. The result is a calm look from reception to feed.

  • 09↗
    Brand Identity · Healthcare

    Aysh Care

    IdentityHealthcareSignage

    A healthcare provider needed an identity that feels caring and modern and stays legible from an app icon to building signage. I designed the wordmark and a figure icon that doubles as the letter Y, with a deep navy, teal and mint palette, applied to app icons, uniforms, outdoor signage and a digital screen ad. The result is one identity that holds at every size.

  • 10↗
    Outdoor Concept · Healthcare

    City Care Hospital

    OutdoorHealthcareArt Direction

    A hospital entrance is passed in seconds, so the message had to land at a glance. I made an outdoor standee concept: the promise “Your health, our priority” over a doctor and patient photo, four trust icons and the appointment line in a high-contrast band at eye level. The result tells people who the hospital is, why to trust it and how to book.
